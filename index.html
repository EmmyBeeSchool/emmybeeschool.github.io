Emmy's Bee School

Little Numbers. Big Discoveries. Happy Little Learners.

Every little discovery begins with a little wonder.

Childhood is filled with magical first moments: the first word, the first step, the first time a child recognizes a number and proudly says, “I know that one!”

At Emmy's Bee School, we believe that learning should feel like an adventure, not a lesson.

That's why we created a cheerful little world where a friendly bee named Emmy invites young children to explore numbers from 1 to 10 through colorful balloons, playful animals, gentle sounds, and delightful surprises.

There are no complicated instructions or stressful tests. Just a welcoming forest, a curious little bee, and the excitement of discovering something new.

A Little Adventure for Children. A Little Breathing Room for Parents.

We know that raising a young child is a beautiful journey — but also a busy and sometimes exhausting one.

Between preparing meals, tidying the house, and taking care of everyday responsibilities, even a few peaceful minutes can mean a lot.

Emmy's Bee School was created with both children and their families in mind.

While your little one enjoys a cheerful, age-appropriate introduction to numbers, you can have a few moments to take care of everyday tasks nearby, knowing that screen time has a thoughtful educational purpose.

Because sometimes, a little help makes a big difference.

Learning Through Play

With Emmy as their friendly guide, children can:

  • Discover and recognize numbers from 1 to 10.
  • Explore colorful balloons and delightful animal characters.
  • Build familiarity with numbers through repetition and playful surprises.
  • Enjoy a gentle, welcoming learning environment.
  • Learn at their own pace, one little discovery at a time.

Designed especially for young beginners, Emmy's Bee School makes early number recognition feel natural, enjoyable, and rewarding.

Made for Little Learners. Created with Families in Mind.

We believe the best educational experiences bring together curiosity, kindness, and joy.

Emmy's Bee School is our small contribution to those precious early years — a place where learning begins with a smile.

For every little learner discovering something new.

And for every loving parent who deserves a little moment of calm.

A Little Secret for Parents

Need to return to the main menu? Look for the small white flower above and to the left of the tree hollow.

🌼 Hold for 3 seconds

Touch and hold the upper white flower for at least 3 seconds to return to the game's main menu.

This hidden shortcut helps prevent little explorers from accidentally leaving their adventure.

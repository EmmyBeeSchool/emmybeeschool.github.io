Privacy Policy

Emmy's Bee School | NICKSESS Theater

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

Our commitment to families: Emmy's Bee School is an early-learning game designed for young children. We aim to minimize data processing and provide a safe, age-appropriate learning experience.

1. Who We Are

Emmy's Bee School is developed and operated by Mykola Kubryakov under the creative brand NICKSESS Theater. In this policy, "we," "us," and "our" refer to the developer and operator of Emmy's Bee School.

Privacy questions may be sent to support@relaxess.app.

2. Information Used by the App

The game does not require children to create an account or provide their name, email address, or telephone number to play.

The free version stores gameplay-time information locally on the device to manage the daily 15-minute limit. This information includes the local calendar day and time used. It is used to operate the game and is not intended to identify a child.

3. Subscriptions and Purchase Information

Emmy's Bee School offers an optional Premium subscription for $2.99 per month, providing unlimited gameplay time and access to all three levels.

In-app purchases are processed through the applicable platform store, such as Apple's App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status, purchases, and restoration of purchases.

RevenueCat may process a generated app-user identifier, subscription entitlements, transaction-related identifiers, purchase history, and technical information necessary to provide and maintain subscription functionality. We do not ask children to enter payment-card details directly into the game.

For more information, please review RevenueCat's Privacy Policy , as well as the privacy information of the applicable app store.

4. Children's Privacy

Emmy's Bee School is intended for young children and their families. We do not require children to submit personal contact information to use the learning activities.

Subscription purchases and account management are intended to be handled by a parent or legal guardian through the applicable app-store account.

If you believe personal information about a child has been provided to us improperly, please contact us so that we can investigate and respond appropriately.

5. Advertising and Analytics

The current app design does not include third-party advertising or an independently integrated behavioral analytics service. Technical data may nevertheless be processed by the operating system, app store, and subscription-service provider as described in this policy.

6. Device Permissions

The learning activities use visual animations and audio playback. The app is not designed to record a child's voice or to access the device camera, contacts, or precise location for gameplay.

7. Data Sharing

We use third-party services where necessary to deliver subscriptions and purchases. Relevant purchase and technical information may be processed by RevenueCat and the applicable app-store provider.

We do not sell children's personal information.

8. Data Retention and Your Choices

Gameplay-time records are stored on the device and may be removed when local application data is cleared or the app is uninstalled, subject to the device's backup and restoration behavior.

Subscription-related information may be retained by RevenueCat and the applicable app store according to their respective policies and legal obligations.

Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions or requests concerning information processed through our services. We will review such requests and explain any limitations involving third-party records or legally required retention.

9. Security

We seek to limit the information needed to operate the game and use established platform and subscription services. No electronic system can guarantee absolute security.

10. International Processing

Third-party providers supporting subscriptions and app distribution may process technical or purchase information in countries other than the user's country. Their privacy policies describe relevant practices.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its services, or applicable requirements change. The effective date displayed above will be updated when appropriate.

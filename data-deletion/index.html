How to Request Data Deletion

Parents or legal guardians may request deletion of personal data associated with Emmy's Bee School.

  1. Email support@relaxess.app.
  2. Use the subject: Emmy's Bee School - Data Deletion Request.
  3. Describe your request and provide only information necessary to help identify relevant subscription records. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or a child's personal information.
  4. We will review the request, explain any additional information needed, and assist with deletion of eligible records.

What Data Can Be Deleted?

Gameplay-time information is stored locally on the device. It can generally be removed by clearing the app's local data or uninstalling the app, subject to device backup and restoration behavior.

Subscription-related identifiers and records processed through RevenueCat may be eligible for deletion after the relevant records are identified.

What Data May Be Retained?

Purchase and transaction records may be retained by Apple, Google Play, RevenueCat, or other relevant providers where required for legal, financial, security, or subscription-management purposes. We will explain applicable limitations when responding to a request. Retention periods depend on the provider and applicable requirements.
